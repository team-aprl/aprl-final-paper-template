\section{Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem}
Define the task, the inputs available to your method, and the output you
want to produce. State the assumptions and constraints explicitly.
Introduce notation before using it, and remove mathematics that does not
help explain your particular problem.

For formatting purposes only, let $x\in\mathcal{X}$ denote an input and
$\hat{y}\in\mathcal{Y}$ an estimated output. A generic mapping is
\begin{equation}
  \hat{y}=f_{\theta}(x),
  \label{eq:mapping}
\end{equation}
where $\theta$ denotes the parameters of the mapping. Replace
Eq.~\eqref{eq:mapping} and these definitions with your own formulation.
Specify how success will be measured and under which conditions.
