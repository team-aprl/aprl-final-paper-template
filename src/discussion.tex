\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
Interpret your findings in relation to the research question and prior work.
Discuss alternative explanations, failure cases, and limits of the data
and evaluation. Address ethical considerations where relevant, and identify
a concrete next step for resolving the most important limitation.

\subsection{Scope and Generalization}
Explain how the study's conditions differ from the setting in which the
method might ultimately be used. Identify assumptions about inputs,
available resources, and user behavior that were not directly evaluated.
Discuss whether the observed evidence could plausibly transfer to other
conditions and describe the additional study needed to test that transfer.
Do not treat a limited evaluation as proof of general applicability.

\subsection{Practical Considerations and Future Work}
Prioritize one or two follow-up studies. Explain which unresolved question
each study addresses, what evidence it would collect, and how that evidence
could change the present conclusion. A useful future-work paragraph states
a testable next step rather than promising unspecified improvements.
