\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Summarize the problem, your idea, and the conclusion supported by your
evidence. End with a specific direction for future work.

Connect the main finding to the research question introduced at the start
of the paper. Explain what the completed work adds to the reader's
understanding and which assumptions limit that interpretation. Distinguish
demonstrated outcomes from expected benefits. Identify one concrete next
study that would strengthen or challenge the conclusion, and explain why
that study matters. Avoid introducing new results or unsupported claims here.
